4 · Watched, rated, saved to rewatch

One bug to fix

Oppenheimer

2023 · 3h · Drama   Watched · you rated it 9/10

Watch again

This already mostly works. Rating a movie puts it in Library under Watched. You can add it to My list as well, and Play starts from the beginning. Finishing it again sends a second play to Simkl. The proposal is to say "Watch again" instead of "Play" once you've finished it. The bug: if you save a movie right after rating it, before Krida's next Simkl pull, Krida doesn't know Simkl has it yet. It queues "add to Plan to watch", so Simkl moves your watched movie back to Plan to watch. The fix is to also check Krida's own watched record before queuing that.
