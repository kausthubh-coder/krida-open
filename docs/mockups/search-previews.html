A. Posters that open in place, like Home (recommended)

The grid keeps posters. Resting on one widens it into its trailer exactly as on Home: same spring, same card. Its row's neighbours slide right (past the edge) and come back when you leave. One control line replaces the two chip rows.

B. Landscape cards, trailer plays inside the card

Results become 16:9 backdrop cards in four columns. Nothing moves when a trailer starts; it fades in over the backdrop. Fewer titles per screen (12 instead of 14 to 21), and regional films without a backdrop fall back to a cropped poster.
